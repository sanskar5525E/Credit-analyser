What CreditPulse Does

Everything Your Team Needs

From raw invoice data to actionable decisions in seconds.

📊
Risk Scoring Engine
Every customer scored A–D based on overdue days, outstanding ratio, and payment behaviour. FMCG India specific — 45 day cap.
🔮
Behaviour Predictor
Analyses invoice history to predict if a customer will pay late next time. Improving, Worsening, or Stable — before the invoice is even due.
📞
Call Priority Table
Grade D customers first. Ready-made call scripts with customer name and outstanding amount filled in. Log outcomes directly.
📅
Ageing Report
Current / 1-15 / 16-30 / 31-45 / 45+ day buckets per customer. Exactly what your CA needs every quarter. Export to Excel.
🗄️
Permanent History
All invoice data stored in a real database. Every upload builds on the last. Behaviour predictor gets more accurate over time.
🔒
Private Per Business
Each wholesaler has their own login. Your data is completely separate from other businesses. No one else can see your customers.
